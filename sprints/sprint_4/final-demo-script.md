# AgentGuard Final Demo Script

Target length: 15-20 minutes.

1. Introduce AgentGuard and the AI-agent governance problem.
2. Summarize four-sprint delivery journey.
3. Show architecture: React, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Alembic, policy engine, approval workflow, MCP/AI adapters, audit trail.
4. Demonstrate role switching.
5. Demonstrate policy simulation: allow, approval-required, deny.
6. Run governed AI-agent prompt.
7. Review approval queue and approve/reject a sensitive action.
8. Show execution evidence and correlation ID.
9. Show audit trail and export.
10. Show AI explanation and clarify that AI never overrides deterministic policy decisions.
11. Show release readiness screen.
12. Show GitHub, Jira board, CI checks, tests, and release tags.
13. Discuss limitations, security decisions, and future work.
14. Close with final submission links and acknowledgements.
